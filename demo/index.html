    Keyboard, swipe and tap zones

    Turn pages by hand: ← → PgUp PgDn, Home/End jump to the book's start/end; swipe on touch; or tap the left / right third of the page (hover to see the zones). Tap a book image to zoom it full-screen (Esc or a backdrop click closes). Select text to get a menu anchored on the selection; tap a highlight to get its menu (a tap on a highlight never turns the page). Tap anywhere else to dismiss. RTL books flip left/right. Click into the page first so it has focus.
